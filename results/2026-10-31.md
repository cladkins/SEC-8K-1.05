### Disclosure

> Other Events. Revolve Group, Inc. (the “Company”) recently became aware of unauthorized access to certain of its systems.
>
> The Company promptly initiated its cyber incident response protocols and confirmed the unauthorized activity was remediated. The Company proceeded to assess and investigate the scope of the incident and to identify and implement enhanced security measures designed to prevent a similar event from occurring. The Company also notified federal law enforcement authorities and engaged leading external cybersecurity experts to assist with the investigation and response efforts.
>
> Based on its investigation, the Company believes that an unauthorized actor may have obtained, through automated means, the name, contact information, payment card number and payment card expiration date for certain customers who saved payment information associated with their online shopping account on the Company’s e-commerce platform. This incident has not resulted in operational disruption or interrupted service to any customers, and as of the date of this report, the Company does not believe this incident will have a material impact on its business.
